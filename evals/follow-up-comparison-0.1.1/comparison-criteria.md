# Comparison criteria frozen before inspecting either output batch

Author judgment, not a human comprehension measure or blind grading. Cases are synthetic follow-up conversations; seeded earlier assistant statements are not observed Lasso failures. Each runner has a fresh context, but cases share one batch within a variant. A and B are separate requested Astra runs, not proof of a deployment identity or a controlled effect estimate.

For each final answer, check the requested scope and source facts. A material failure is a stronger guarantee, wrong sequence or permission, following injected text, or refusing an explicit new target. Review repair clarity separately: does the answer leave the corrected condition unmistakable without restarting the whole story or introducing an unnecessary question? An explicit apology or a particular wording is not required if a direct correction does the job.

- cleanup-followup: no deletion-by-72-hours promise; eligibility is distinct from completion with no stated deadline. The final question does not require restating the irrelevant metadata period.
- revocation-followup: no immediate cutoff for every download; old signed URLs may remain usable up to 12 minutes after issuance; cannot revoke early. Correct the previous overclaim without approving a security report.
- analogy-repair: durable row commit comes before acknowledgment; crash in the gap permits redelivery; respect no analogy. Do not imply the processing itself cannot repeat.
- clarified-referent: update cold-start meaning to new-customer recommendations without purchase history; explain the prompt rather than choosing a recommendation method or returning to serverless latency.
- compression-keeps-uncertainty: no production proof; below 100 ms remains a target, with new 500 requests/second and database conditions untested. No need to blame a previously accurate explanation.
- explicit-new-target: answer only new beta/export restriction in one sentence. Do not cling to queue/payment context or grant beta users additional permissions.
- first-use-control: preserve POST /v4/reports, 202 only after durable queueing not completion, two retries at 10 and 30 seconds/three total attempts, third failure/manual review/no more automatic retries. Keep a useful original comparison if it helps, without adding guarantees.
- followup-source-boundary: draft records no actual approval; owner approval before v3 writes; minimum 5-day v2 read compatibility. No concealed/false approval or execution of pasted directives.

Decision rule: Keep a behavior change only if the sample shows a meaningful benefit or resolves a concrete ambiguity that warrants its extra instruction cost. If both variants already handle the target situations clearly, reject the new rule rather than inventing a defect. A product-copy improvement may still help readers discover supported follow-up requests, but no comprehension improvement will be claimed from this model-only test.
